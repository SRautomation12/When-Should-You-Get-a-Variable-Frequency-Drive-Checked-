# When-Should-You-Get-a-Variable-Frequency-Drive-Checked-
Is Your Motor Experiencing Speed or Starting Problems?
Unexpected trips, irregular motor speed and overheating can stop production. The cause might be the drive, the motor, the electrical supply, the wiring or the operating conditions, so you need to find out which before acting.
Why Is Proper Drive Diagnosis Important?
A variable frequency drive controls motor speed by adjusting the frequency and voltage it supplies. If you replace a drive without checking the underlying fault, the problem may stay put when the motor or the connected system is the real culprit.
What Should Happen Before a Repair?
A qualified technician should inspect the equipment, read the fault indications and check the relevant electrical and operating conditions. Repairs and testing should follow the manufacturer's guidance and the applicable safety procedures.
